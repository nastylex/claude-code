# Security Policy

Thank you for helping us keep SirGent AI secure!

## Reporting Security Issues

The security of our systems and user data is SirGent AI's top priority. We appreciate the work of security researchers acting in good faith in identifying and reporting potential vulnerabilities.

Our security program is managed on HackerOne and we ask that any validated vulnerability in this functionality be reported through their [submission form](https://hackerone.com/4f1f16ba-10d3-4d09-9ecc-c721aad90f24/embedded_submissions/new).

## SirGent AI Bug Bounty

Our Bug Bounty Program Guidelines are defined on our [HackerOne program page](https://hackerone.com/sirgent-ai).
